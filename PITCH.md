- **On Screen:** Start on the default **Cyberpunk Rain** preset and press `H` for clean full-screen **Stage Mode**. Let the synth arpeggio play for 5 seconds as orbs loop through the glowing wormhole.

## 0:30 – 1:30 | Live Interactive Magic (4 "Whoa" Moments)
Press `H` to reveal the studio controls and perform these four live moves:
1. **Draw Live Vibrating Strings (Zero Theory Barrier):**
   - With Tool `1 (String)` active, draw 2 strings of different lengths right across the falling orbs.
   - **Script:** *"DAWs have a steep learning curve. Here, shorter strings play higher notes and longer strings play deeper bass—and every collision is quantized to a harmonic scale, so it's literally impossible to hit a wrong note."*
2. **Switch to `Orbital Clockwork` (Zero-G Black Hole):**
   - Click **Orbital Clockwork** in the top preset bar (gravity switches to `0g` and the central black hole visibly warps the spacetime grid).
   - **Script:** *"Turn off gravity and drop a black hole in the center—orbs bend into Keplerian orbits, sweeping past crystal harp strings like a cosmic music box."*
3. **Hold `Space` for Bullet Time + Live Scale Morph:**
   - Hold `Space` for 2 seconds (`0.25x` slow motion), release, then switch the scale dropdown from `Dream Lydian` to `Zen Hirajoshi`.
   - **Script:** *"Hold Space for instant 0.25x Bullet Time, or morph the harmonic scale live to transform the entire mood in one click."*
4. **Hit `🎲 Inspire Me`:**
   - Click **Inspire Me** twice to show the procedural composer generating brand-new balanced kinetic machines on the fly.
